I found no blocker or major defect in T34. R8, R9 and P11 each pass, and my verdict is **pass**, with two minor findings.

**What I checked**
- **R8 (pairing within the configured days):** `firm.py` reads `own_account_pairing_days`. It defaults to 3, accepts whole numbers from 0 to 31 and refuses a true/false value. `overrides.py` passes the value into the pairing code, and `pack.py` uses the same value for the preview and records it in the preview's snapshot.
- **R9 (a debit with more than one candidate):** such a debit is not paired. It comes back as a group with every candidate, and that group reaches both the workspace queue and the pack's queue.
- **P11:** the 20 invariant tests named `p11` all ran and passed in the runner's logs.
- **Edge cases I tried:**
  - Two debits wanting the same single credit are both left unpaired.
  - A window of 0 days pairs same-day moves only.
  - Changing the days after a preview makes sign-off refuse it, naming the setting.
  - A bad value refuses the figures in the workspace and in `cma case figures`.
  - Which lines pair does not depend on the order the accounts are given in.
  - An overridden line, a protected line, a returned direct debit or a reversal is never paired.

**Minor findings**
1. **P11 is proven only on cases where no amounts coincide** (`tests/test_own_account_pairing.py:138`). The test's own generator gives every line a distinct amount, except a move's two halves. A £50.00 card settlement into one account and a £50.00 council direct debit from another, two days apart, would be paired as an own-account transfer. That is what R8's literal wording asks for. But then total money in less own-account transfers in no longer equals the outside credits, so P11's third clause fails. That is a conflict between R8 and P11, which the owner should settle in the spec. A test exercising it would also make it visible.
2. **`files_changed` in claim.json is incomplete.** It lists only two test files and the README. The diff also changes `src/cma/firm.py`, `src/cma/overrides.py`, `src/cma/pack.py` and `src/cma/categorise.py`.

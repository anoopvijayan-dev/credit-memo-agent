**What changed**
- **Default ruleset:** v2 is now the default when `CMA_RULESET` is unset. `CMA_RULESET=v1` still loads the frozen v1 table, so the rollback flag works.
- **Committed example:** regenerated under v2. Re-running `cma run` on the CSV reproduces the committed memo (apart from the approval time) and every key of its evidence.
  - The CSV is byte-identical.
  - The director capital line (£8,000) moves from unexplained money in (M25) to director funding (M20), and M26 drops from 2.5% to 0.0%.
  - M1–M16 don't move.
  - The appendix carries M1–M28, with M16 under its new name.
- **Wording:**
  - R9 in the packaged policy now reads "No more than 20% of money in that is not trading receipts". This changes the policy file's hash.
  - The offline model's M16 sentence now reads "Money in that is not trading receipts was … [M16]".
- **MCP:** a new read-only tool, `money_in_buckets`, returns total money in (M17), the eight buckets that add up to it (M2, M19–M25) and the cash inside trading receipts (M18). Each comes with line ids only, never descriptions. It uses the same data-directory path check as the other tools and refuses while the offline lock is on.
- **Lines v2 newly recognises:** listed in `docs/v2-newly-recognised.md` and linked from the README. A test checks the page against the list the P7 dual-run test recomputes from the committed corpus.

**Existing tests I changed**
- **`test_categorise`:** the HMRC VAT repayment credit is now expected as `hmrc_repayment`. It is one of the listed lines.
- **Two `test_rules` tests:** these imitate checkpoints from releases that categorised with v1, so they now draft with `CMA_RULESET=v1`.
- **`test_synth_features` (from T10):** this test pinned the hashes of the generator's truth files.
  - The design's Migration section says the director capital line's truth becomes `director_funding`, so the generator now always writes that.
  - The test still pins every CSV hash, and still matches the old truth hash once exactly those capital lines are mapped back to `other`. The golden file is untouched.
- **`test_p7_history` rollback test:** it now drafts under `CMA_RULESET=v1` before the previous release resumes the review. A review drafted under v2 can hold a category the previous release doesn't have (`director_funding` here), and the previous release crashes reading it back.

**Decision for the owner:** rolling back to the previous wheel only works for pending reviews drafted under v1, so set `CMA_RULESET=v1` before drafting anything that might need it. The design promises that old checkpoints resume in this release, not the reverse. I've raised this in the claim's notes; whether it needs documenting or a stated contract is the owner's call.

### Summary of findings

1. **Major ([verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L247-L289), R44 / P13):** `_verify_pack` checks only statement hashes listed in `inputs["statements"]`, then calls `overrides.case_figures(folder, ...)` which loads *every* account in the case log ([overrides.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/overrides.py#L385-L414)). Importing a second account into a case *after* signing off a 1-account pack leaves all recorded inputs intact, yet `verify` pulls the new account into `pair_own_accounts` (mutating the signed account's recomputed figures) and reports `Mismatch("account <new>", "not recorded", "recomputed")` (exit 1) instead of recomputing solely from the recorded accounts.
2. **Major ([verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L118), R44):** `_compare` ([verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L118)) and `_compare_months` ([verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L142-L148)) use plain Python `!=`, where `True == 1.0`, `False == 0.0`, `0 == False`, and `1 == True`. Tampering numeric metrics/cells with booleans (e.g., `M7.value = true`, `M13.value = false`, `days_overdrawn.value = false`) or `part_month` with `0`/`1` silently passes verification with zero mismatches.
3. **Major ([verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L305-L334), R44 / claim #1):** Several malformed evidence record shapes bypass `_check_shape` and crash `verify()` / `cma verify` with unhandled exceptions (exit 1 traceback) or silent wrong slicing instead of `VerifyRefused` (exit 2):
   - `{"accounts": null}` crashes at [verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L318) (`TypeError: 'NoneType' object is not iterable`).
   - Non-dict monthly cells (e.g., `"money_in": 500.0` or `"500.00"`) crash at [verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L147-L148) (`TypeError`/`AttributeError`).
   - Non-dict `inputs.statements` / `inputs.override_log` crash at [verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L247) and [verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L261); non-int `inputs.own_account_pairing_days` crashes in `pair_own_accounts` ([categorise.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/categorise.py#L1524)); negative or boolean `inputs.override_log.position` (`-1`, `True`, `False`) passes `isinstance(position, int)` at [verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L266) and silently slices `records[:override_position]` at [overrides.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/overrides.py#L388-L392) (and non-int/null silently falls back to `None`, applying post-sign-off overrides).
   - Malformed `money_in.total` / `money_in.cash_within_trading` (e.g., `{"id": ["M17"]}` or `"bad"`) either crashes with `TypeError: unhashable type: 'list'` at [verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L113) or reports a mismatch (exit 1) instead of refusing (exit 2).
4. **Minor ([verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L200), R44):** `_verify_run` skips the statement SHA-256 check whenever `statement.sha256` is `null`, `""`, or absent, allowing a tampered v1 `cma run` record with a stripped hash to verify against any substituted statement file.
5. **Minor ([verify.py](file:///home/user/credit-memo-agent/.bridgie/worktrees/statement-review/T38-review/src/cma/verify.py#L108-L162), R44):** `_compare`, `_compare_months`, and `_verify_pack` do not detect duplicate metric ids, bucket ids, months, or accounts (and `_buckets` flattens `total`/`buckets`/`cash_within_trading`), so duplicated identical entries verify with zero mismatches and inflated `checked` counts.

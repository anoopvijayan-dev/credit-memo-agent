I've written revision 21 of the spec. It covers D168 and takes in the two latest failed reviews: verify-80 (T12 at 1e35352) and verify-81 (T03 at 5139e22). Only R4 and R42 change, and every id, form and risk tag stays the same.

**R42**
- **One answer per report.** Every report a save's writer gets gives one answer, and that includes the errors it carries or was raised from.
- **Not-confirmed reports.** When the take-back is refused, the report names what stopped it in the save's own words and does not quote the refusal. No part of it says the save was recorded, was not recorded, or that nothing was written. This answers verify-80's first major.
- **Not-recorded reports.** These never say the save was recorded or is not confirmed.
- **Published or not.** Whether a head record was published is decided by whether its rename ran, not by reading the head record back. So a head record that can't be read before its rename answers "not recorded". This answers verify-80's second major.

**R4**
- **Exception check.** Only v1 with its pinned bytes skips it. A changed v1 is refused as changed first, then names each rule that carries an exception. This answers verify-81's first major.
- **Changed v1 otherwise.** Apart from that check, a changed v1 is judged as the kind of table it says it is. A copy that still calls itself frozen isn't held to stages or the category order, which v1's own rules lack. This matches what the owner's 5f5bb2e does and stops a reviewer applying the "every other table" clause to changed copies of v1.
- **Unusable patterns.** Every rule's matchers are built while the table is checked. A pattern that compiles alone but can't be used is named alongside the table's other faults, not only after they are fixed. This answers verify-81's second major.

P15 and R54 don't change, and nothing in the design needs a decision from the owner this time.

The owner's three new commits (26bc51e, 7fb3a23, 5f5bb2e) haven't been reviewed yet. Reading them, two gaps would likely fail the next reviews:
- **T12:** a save refused before any part of its line is written is now told only "the step was refused". R42 asks for "not recorded".
- **T03:** a rule with an unusable pattern and another malformed field (say, an `unless` given as a list) has only the malformed field named. The pattern problem shows up only after that field is fixed.

**What changed**
- **How a credit is decided** (`src/cma/categorise.py`):
  - Every matching rule is collected. A match inside a longer listed phrase counts only as the longer one, so STRIPE inside STRIPE CAPITAL drops out.
  - UNPAID DD at the start of the line settles it on its own.
  - Otherwise the line-type cues decide if any cue with a bucket matches; if none does, the name lists decide.
  - Two or more buckets make the credit ambiguous. So does a payment rail (Faster Payment, BACS, a transfer) together with cash wording. An ambiguous credit is unexplained money in, and its rule id lists every candidate (`v2:conflict:a+b`).
  - The order is: a per-line override, then a protected verdict, then a profile name, then an ambiguous verdict, then firm rules, then an unprotected verdict, then the default. So a firm rule can never settle an ambiguous credit.
  - Debits keep first-match order, and v1 is untouched.
- **Rule table** (`src/cma/rules/v2.yaml`):
  - I removed the HMRC rule's savings-wording exception and the refund rule's HMRC exception.
  - HMRC REFUND phrases are now on the HMRC list, and DIRECTOR LOAN phrases on the director list.
  - The bare pot-label rule is gone, and cash paid in is no longer protected.
  - The payment rail is a new cue with no category.
  - The only exception left is cash excluding cheques. A test checks that.

**New tests**
- A shuffle test reorders the rules within each stage with 8 seeds. It checks the same bucket, and for ambiguous lines the same candidate set, on every credit case and every listed name.
- A pairwise matrix crosses every credit list (HMRC and returned DD included) with every other. It checks that each pair matches exactly what its two parts match alone, and that the verdict follows R4. This answers the minor finding that the old matrix had no HMRC cue.

I accepted the other two minor findings. The 10,000-line test times only categorisation, so I make no claim about the 2-second recompute target. The earlier note about M9/M16 was wrong, and I've withdrawn it.

**Decisions for the owner**
- **Cash vs card category.** When two lists in the same bucket match, the design records the first in table order, so the category depends on order. `COUNTER DEPOSIT SUMUP` comes out as cash rather than card, which affects M18 (cash) and M15 (customer transfers). The bucket doesn't change. Should these be ambiguous instead?
- **Cue plus rail and cash.** I let a cue with a bucket (such as HMRC) decide even when a rail and cash wording are also present. The rail-with-cash rule only applies when the name lists decide.
- **Newly ambiguous lines.** Some credit lines that used to resolve are now ambiguous: a pot move with HMRC wording, `CLEARPAY FINANCE LTD`, `COUNCIL GRANT INV 12`, `J SMITH SHAREHOLDER INV 1234`, and cash wording on a rail. None of these is in the committed fixtures, and M1–M16 there don't change.

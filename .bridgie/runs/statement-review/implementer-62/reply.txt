T25 is built and the full suite passes (2,425 tests). At sign-off the pack now writes `monthly.csv` alongside `memo.md` and the fuller `evidence.json`. Nothing is committed.

**evidence.json (v1)** now also records:
- **Accounts:** for each account, its import format, the input file's hash, its period, line count and money-in buckets with their lines.
- **Overrides:** every override with its status, and the override log position.
- **Hashes:** firm rules, thresholds and profile, at the top level.
- **Policy check:** when hidden, `shown: false`, `hidden: true` and the policy hash. When shown, the label, policy name and hash as before.

**Today's keys:** `cma run`'s evidence record still has exactly its 17 keys, and its `policy` key is unchanged. Each pack account carries the same per-statement keys in the same shapes. A test checks that, for the same statement, `cma run` and the pack give the same values, the policy result included.

**monthly.csv:** one row per account and month. Each cell has its value to the penny (days as whole numbers, empty when n/a), its note and the ids of its lines. Every text cell has a leading `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab or carriage return neutralised with an apostrophe (P8). The test uses line ids like `=HYPERLINK(1)` and `+SUM(A1)`. The file's hash is recorded in the pack-write and sign-off entries.

Three choices the verifier may question:
- **Numbers aren't neutralised.** The value columns are numbers, not text cells, so a negative balance is written as `-12.34` with no apostrophe. Adding one would make the spreadsheet treat it as text.
- **Not every `cma run` key is at the pack's top level.** A pack can hold several accounts, so the per-statement keys sit under each account. `redrafts` and `review` don't appear at all: a pack has no redrafts, and its review is the `sign_off` key.
- **Line ids are space-separated in the CSV.** An id can itself contain a space; `evidence.json` lists them exactly.

Changes are in `src/cma/pack.py`, with new tests in `tests/test_pack_evidence.py`. Three existing tests in `tests/test_signoff.py` now expect `monthly.csv` in the pack.

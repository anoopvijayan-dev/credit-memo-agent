I fixed the main bug the reviewer found in the Open Banking importer. One of their minor cases is fixed too; the other two still fail as before. Each fix has a new test, and lint, the contracts tier and the full suite all pass. The changes are not committed; they sit in the worktree on top of f32f8d7.

- **Major (fixed):** when lines at one moment had no balance, the importer could pick a wrong opening balance and then refuse a valid file as a broken chain. The cause was in `_order`: a group of one line was accepted without checking that its balance follows from the one before it. It is now checked.
  - The reviewer's three-line example now imports in the right order, from £100.00 to £107.00.
  - A single line that genuinely doesn't follow is still refused with the same "the balance walked" message as before.
- **Minor, search limit (partly fixed):** if any later group has a balance on every line, it fixes where the walk must start. `_first_order` now tries only those starts, so the reviewer's case with 17 balance-less lines now imports instead of being refused at the search limit.
  - **Still open:** a valid file can still be refused at the search limit in two cases:
    - the first group has more than 16 balance-less lines and no later group has a balance on every line;
    - a gap needs more than `MEET_LIMIT` (1 << 16) ways to try.

    The refusal says the file may be valid and never calls it a broken chain. Removing these refusals completely would need a search with no time limit, which the design doesn't settle. That is the owner's call, and it is in the claim's notes.
- **Minor, last claim (corrected):** the previous claim had the wrong `ORDER_STEPS` value, called a test an xfail when it has no xfail mark, and cited proofs from /tmp scripts. This claim only quotes commands I ran in the worktree, with their real output.

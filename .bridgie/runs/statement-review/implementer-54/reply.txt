T22 is built and passes all its tests. The underwriter can now change a line's category from the workspace and confirm or drop stale overrides there. At 10,000 lines the recomputed figures come back in under 2 seconds. Nothing is committed or marked done; the verifier decides.

**What the underwriter sees**
- **Override form:** every line in the judgement queue and in each drill-down has a form. It offers only the categories valid for the line's direction, and needs a reason and a typed name.
- **Saves:** each save goes into the override log and the case log. The reply carries the recomputed figures (marked where they rest on an override), the indicators and the policy check.
- **Conflict and reload:** each form carries the case log position the page was built at. A save from an out-of-date page is refused with a 409 that says to reload, and records nothing.
- **Stale overrides:** they are listed on the page with confirm and drop buttons. One whose line has gone can only be dropped, and one that isn't stale is refused.
- **Refusals:** anything other than a category change (amount, date, balance, description, adding or removing a line) is refused, naming the field. So are a missing reason, name, position, line or category, and an unknown line or category. None of these appends anything.

**The 2-second target (R52):** a first build took about 2.5 s per save at 10,000 lines. To get under the target I added caches in `overrides.py`, keyed by content hashes: the parsed statement file, each line's category without overrides, and the figures without overrides. A changed statement, profile or rule table never hits a stale entry, and the figures with overrides are still recomputed on every request. On this machine a save now takes about 0.07 s and the recomputed summary 0.6–0.9 s. The timed tests use a generated 10,000-line statement; this has not been checked on a typical office laptop.

What revision 25 changes (ids, forms and risk tags unchanged):
- **R2:** amounts and balances are read exactly from the statement text and counted in whole pence. A statement is refused at import, naming the line or saying which total is too big, when any of these is £10,000,000,000,000 or more:
  - one of its amounts or balances, whether in credit or overdrawn;
  - its money in or its money out.

  Below that limit every money figure is reported to the penny. One exception is written in: average monthly revenue (M3) over a period shorter than a month can be bigger than trading receipts, and stays n/a with the reason, as T04 already builds it.
- **P2, R10 and R54:** M1–M16 keep the evidence the previous release gave them, which the dual run already compares. So M6 cites the line that closed each overdrawn day and M5 the line that set the lowest balance. The new derived figures (M26–M28) and the monthly balance and overdrawn-day cells cite every line their calculation reads. The term *derived figure* is restated to match. D223 said "R1/R54"; I read that as R10 and R54, because those are the requirements that pin M1–M16 and R1 covers only the new buckets. The revision note says so, and R1 is unchanged.
- **R11:** cites D220 (money out is an outflow figure) and says which lines its balance and overdrawn-day cells cite.
- **Design:** a new Context section on verify-129 and why the limit is £10,000,000,000,000. Fifteen significant digits always survive a float, so below that limit nothing is lost, no total is withheld and no displayed figure is off by a penny. There are also two new sub-decisions and edits across the rest of the design, including five new failure-mode rows and consumer-trace item 26.

Two things are still open in T04 at 0c63ca4 that this revision doesn't fix (both are proposed as tasks):
- **Monzo importer:** it still reads amounts as floats, so `cma import monzo` doesn't refuse at the limit as R2 now requires.
- **R3:** the early refusal still names only the credits that have no category, not the full difference and every line involved.

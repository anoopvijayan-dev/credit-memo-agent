**Minor findings:**
1. **A provider category can refuse the import** (`src/cma/importers/truelayer.py:220`). The check for a repeated transaction compares whole objects. Two pages that repeat a transaction and differ only in `transaction_category` are refused as "repeated with different content". This contradicts the importer's own note that a hint never refuses the file. The OBIE importer refuses any differing repeat the same way, so this is consistent with R36, but the hint fields could be left out of the comparison.
2. **The amount check is stricter than OBIE's** (`src/cma/importers/truelayer.py:80`). It allows at most two decimal places, so a whole-pence number written with a trailing zero, such as `-12.400`, is refused. The OBIE importer accepts it.

**The new major (R29, P18):** the lock handling is installed by assigning `invoke`, `stream`, `ainvoke` and `astream` on the compiled graph object (`graph.py:766`). The installed LangGraph's `with_config()` builds a new graph through `copy()` (`langgraph/pregel/main.py:1000-1009`). The copy's constructor silently swallows those four attributes (`main.py:790`), so the copy runs the plain LangGraph methods. On that copy:

- A resume does not read `firm.yaml` as it resumes, and records no `resume_offline_lock`.

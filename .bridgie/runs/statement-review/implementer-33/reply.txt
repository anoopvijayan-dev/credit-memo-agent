I've built the monthly summary, added it to the evidence record `cma run` writes, and committed it as 2c47748. All four new tests pass, and so do the full suite, every marker tier, ruff, compileall and the offline evals.

- **The summary:** `metrics.monthly_summary()` gives one row per calendar month in the period. Each row says whether it is a part month and which of its days fall in the period. It has money in, money out, trading receipts (cash deposits included), average and lowest end-of-day balance, and days overdrawn. Each cell carries its kind, its evidence ids and, for money cells, the signed total of its lines.
  - Money in and trading receipts are sum cells: their lines add up to the value.
  - Money out is reported as a positive amount, and its lines add up to that amount negated.
  - The two balance cells and days overdrawn cite the line that set the close of every day of the month, including days with no lines, which carry the previous close.
  - A month's average balance is n/a with a reason if it can't be reported to the penny.
- **Evidence record:** it gains one new key, `monthly_summary`. It is computed from the stored lines when the record is written, so nothing new is stored in the review checkpoint.
- **Tests** (`tests/test_monthly.py`):
  - The p12 test checks that monthly trading receipts sum to M2 and monthly money in to M17, to the penny. It covers every synthetic case and 60 random statements under v1 and v2, and asserts that the fixtures contain cash deposits.
  - The p2 test checks every monthly cell by its kind.
  - A hand-worked fixture covers part months, a month carried on one balance, overdrawn days and cash deposits.
  - A contracts test checks the new key in the evidence file `cma run` writes.
- **Committed example:** I added the `monthly_summary` key to `examples/cafe_stretched.memo.evidence.json` and changed nothing else in that file. Before adding it I checked that the example's recorded figures match what the code computes today.

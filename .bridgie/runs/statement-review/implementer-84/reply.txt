I've built the comparison entry (T41, R51): the underwriter types their own figure for a memo field or a month, and the workspace shows the difference and lists the lines a person might read differently. The new tests and the full suite pass (3,064 tests), along with the contracts and invariants tiers, ruff and compileall. It is committed on the task branch as 2c05b03.

**What it does**
- **The difference** is the underwriter's figure less the tool's, worked out in the figures layer (`metrics.comparison`): to the penny for money, exactly for percentages and ratios, and n/a when the tool's own figure is n/a.
- **The lines** are that period's cash deposits, unexplained credits (ambiguous ones included), possible own-account transfers and overridden lines, largest amount first, each with why it is listed (`indicators.comparison_lines`). This also works for the "All accounts" figures of a case with several accounts.
- **Where it lives:** `POST /api/compare` for data and `POST /compare` for the page. The case page has a new "Compare with your own figure" form, and the result page escapes all case text. A comparison saves nothing and adds nothing to the case log; a test checks this.
- **Refusals** name only what was asked: a fraction of a penny, an unknown field or month, a judgement field with no figure, or a field that shows several figures with none chosen.

**Choices I made where R51 was open**
- A memo field is compared on one of the figures the memo field map gives it, over the whole statement period. A month is compared on one of its monthly-summary cells, over the month's days in the period.
- "Unexplained lines" means unexplained credits, as the judgement queue reads them.
- The combined figures don't carry the possible own-account groups, so I take them from the per-account figures.

**Other changes**
- I changed one existing test: the P8 check in `tests/test_workspace_summary.py` lists which form actions the page may have, and I added `/compare` to that list. Its 25 seeds failed until I did.
- I added a short README note about the comparison.
- The `cma` package wasn't importable here, so I ran `pip install -e` for this worktree before testing.

New tests are in `tests/test_compare.py`; the main new code is `src/cma/review/compare.py`, plus the route changes in `server.py`.

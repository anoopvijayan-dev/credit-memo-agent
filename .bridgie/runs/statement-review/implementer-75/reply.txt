**What changed**
- **Case truth:** `synth.Truth` now has a `case` field, filled only when the feature set includes `two_accounts`. It holds both accounts' lines under their case line ids (`main:T00001`) with each category. It also holds the own-account pairs, debit first, which come from the generator's own moves (matched by day and amount), not from the categoriser. Then the money-in buckets and cash deposits across the case, the money in from outside the applicant's accounts, and each account's indicators.
- **Unchanged truth files:** a truth file without `two_accounts` gets no `case` key, so earlier truth files stay byte-identical.
- **Making the case:** `synth.to_case(rows, truth, work)` makes one demo case in the case store. It imports the business account as `main` plus the reserve account, and sets the generated directors, own accounts and VAT stagger. `cma generate ... --case` calls it, and the README shows the new flag.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_synth_case.py` checks P17 and P11 on these cases through the real case pipeline, for every archetype and profile and for several feature sets, seeds and lengths:
  - **P17:** every line's category, every bucket with its lines, total money in (M17), the cash deposits (M18) and each account's indicators match the truth.
  - **P11:** each pair is one debit and one credit of equal amount in different accounts, and no line is in two pairs. The pairs are exactly the generator's moves, and no debit is left with two candidates. Total money in less own-account transfers in (M17 minus M19) equals the money from outside the accounts.

Inside a case, the pairing settles these moves before the profile's own-account names are read. So each line keeps the category the earlier per-account truth gave it, but its recorded rule becomes `pairing:...` instead of `profile:own_account`.

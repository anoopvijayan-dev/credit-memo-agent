I've added `cma report`, which prints one summary per case and exits 1 when any case is untraced, does not reconcile, or cannot be read. Lint is clean and the full test suite passes (3003 tests), including five new ones for the report.

For each case it shows:
- **Judgement queue:** how many lines it lists, broken down by why each is listed.
- **Indicators:** how many lines each kind of indicator flagged.
- **Overrides:** the applied ones counted by old and new category; stale ones are counted separately.
- **Unexplained share of money in:** M26, taken across all accounts when a case has more than one.
- **Flags:** whether every figure was traced, whether the figures reconcile, and whether the case was signed off.

`--json` prints the same as JSON. The output carries only the random case id, counts, category and indicator names, yes/no flags and that share. It never prints names, the label or business name, descriptions, amounts, override reasons or typed names, refusal text, or any time from the case log. One test seeds all of those as sentinel strings and checks that none appears in either output, and that nothing reads as a duration.

Three choices a reviewer may question:
- **What counts as traced:** a figure or monthly cell passes if it has lines behind it, is n/a with its reason given, or is zero with no line to cite, such as an empty bucket. That follows the design's wording "every figure has evidence or an n/a reason". The zero case is my own addition.
- **Cases that cannot be read:** a case whose log is broken, is being saved, or whose files are refused shows as "could not be read". Traced, reconciled and signed off all show "not known", and it makes the exit non-zero.
- **How the failing-case tests work:** I couldn't build a real case that fails to reconcile or has an untraced figure. So those two exit-code tests force the state by monkeypatching `overrides.case_figures` and `metrics.compute`.

The counts come from the workspace's case summary, so the report shows what the underwriter sees and does no money arithmetic. I haven't committed anything; the runner does that.

**Verdict: `fail`.** T20 meets most of R16, but the drill-down shows no inputs, or the wrong one, for four derived figures (M4, M7, M9, M13).

**What holds up.** I read the code and confirmed in the runner's logs that all 43 tests in `tests/test_drilldown.py` ran and passed.
- **Lines:** every figure, bucket and monthly cell lists exactly its own lines, in order. Each line carries date, description, amount, balance, category and the rule or override that set it (`drilldown.py:46-52`).
- **Totals:** sum figures show their lines' total. Outflow figures (M8, M11, M12, M14 and the money-out cell) show that total labelled as the figure negated. Both use the figures layer's pence (`metrics.py:782-783`, `drilldown.py:55-61`).
- **Derived figures with correct inputs:** M1, M3, M5, M10, M15, M16, M26, M27, M28, and the monthly balance and overdrawn-day cells.
- **M9's part-counted lines** are named.
- **Security:** both new routes go through the token, Host and Origin checks, and case text is escaped.

**What fails R16 ("for a derived figure … it SHALL show the inputs and the method"):**
1. **M4 (major):** the drill-down lists total revenue (M2) as M4's only input (`metrics.py:786`). The trend is actually computed from the earliest and latest three whole months' revenue (`metrics.py:437-441`), and neither quarter total is shown. On a 7-month statement M2 covers all months, so an underwriter can't get from the shown input to the trend.
2. **M13 (major):** late VAT payments get no inputs at all, so the Inputs section doesn't render. The count depends on the VAT stagger group, the quarters' due dates and the collection window, and none of these are shown, nor which quarters were counted late.
3. **M9 (major):** net operating cash flow gets no inputs either. Its value is its operating lines (leaving out non-trading credits) plus a capped offset. Neither the offset nor how much of each part-counted line counts is shown, so the figure can't be rebuilt from the page.
4. **The test doesn't catch this:** `test_p2_every_figure_s_drilldown_lists_exactly_its_lines_and_total_by_kind` only checks that a derived figure's inputs are a list. An empty list passes.

**Minor findings:**
- `drilldown.py:98` reads the case profile again after the figures are computed, outside the refusal handling. A profile that fails to load gets a generic 500 rather than the workspace's refusal message, and a change between the two reads could show an M28 repayment that differs from the one the figure used.
- The producer's performance claim (M17 taking 2.29s for 5,000 lines) comes from a scratchpad script, not from any test tier the runner ran, so it's unproven. Paging the drill-down is left as an open gap, which also touches R52.

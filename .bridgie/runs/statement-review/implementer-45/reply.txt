I've built the T19 workspace case summary and committed it as d6fd5a2 on the T19 branch. The full suite passed (2078 tests) and ruff is clean.

**What it does now**
- **Normal case:** the page and `/api/case` show, for each account, the money-in breakdown, all 28 figures and the monthly summary. Money is shown to the penny, and figures that rest on an override are marked. Everything is computed by the existing figures layer; the new code in `src/cma/review/summary.py` only formats and renders it.
- **Buckets don't reconcile (R3):** no figures are shown for any account in the case. The page reports the difference in pence and lists each line involved: account, id, date, description, amount and category. To make this possible, `overrides.case_figures` now attaches the account and the categorised statement to the reconciliation error before re-raising it.
- **Case can't be read** (broken or busy case log, override log changed by hand, statement or amount refused): no figures, and the page says why.
- **Escaping (P8):** the summary is built into the page on the server, and every piece of text from the case is HTML-escaped there. The P8 test runs 25 random statements whose descriptions, label and business name are full of markup. It checks the page contains no tags the workspace didn't write, and holds each text only in escaped form, both when figures are shown and on the reconciliation report. With escaping switched off, 26 of those tests fail, so the test does catch the problem.

**Things to know**
- No real categorisation path produces a credit outside every bucket, so the R3 tests break reconciliation by patching `overrides.categorise`.
- I tidied the full stop on one refusal message after the full-suite run. I re-ran the touched test files afterwards (526 passed), but not the whole suite.
- **Descriptions show only on the reconciliation report.** On a normal case they don't appear yet; per-line drill-down is T20.
- **Accounts are shown separately.** If one account doesn't reconcile, every account is hidden; combining accounts is T33.

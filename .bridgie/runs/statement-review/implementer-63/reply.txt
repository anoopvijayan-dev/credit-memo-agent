I've built T26: signing off a case now also writes `record.html` into the pack, and a demo case shows its banner on every workspace page. The full suite passes (2442 tests), as do the invariants tier, ruff, compileall and the offline evals. The work is committed as b6cdfae on the T26 branch.

- **Offline and self-contained:** the record holds no URLs, scripts, images, external stylesheets or forms. Every link points to a section inside the file, and a security policy in the page lets it load nothing from outside.
- **Every figure leads to its lines:** each figure, money-in bucket and monthly cell of each account links to a section listing exactly the lines behind it, in order. Values are to the penny, and each section shows the lines' total or the inputs and method, the same as the workspace's drill-down. The record also shows the sign-off (typed name, operating-system user, UTC time, snapshot) and the memo sections, and it never words the sign-off as approving the loan.
- **P8:** hostile descriptions (markup, quotes, ampersands, formula prefixes) appear in the record only escaped.
- **P20:** with `show_policy_verdict` on, every outcome sits in a section that carries the illustrative label, and the recommendation field is empty. With it off, the record holds no outcome word, no label and no rule result.
- **Demo case:** the banner comes first on the workspace's case page and lines page, and the stamp is in `memo.md` and is the first section of the record. A non-demo case shows none of these.

Things you might not expect:
- **The workspace had no demo banner before.** I added it to every page and to the `/api/case` data.
- **Existing tests changed.** The pack now holds four files, so `tests/test_signoff.py`'s file-list checks were updated.
- **Size:** the record lists each figure's lines in full, so it grows with lines × figures. A 3,000-line statement gave a record of about 4 MB.
- **Wording:** the record reuses the workspace's policy panel text, which says "on the figures as they now stand". In a signed record, "the figures as signed" would be more accurate.

To share the record's code with the workspace, I split the drill-down into a part that gathers the lines and a part that renders them. The case-summary renderer can now take in-page links too.

The new code is in `src/cma/record.py` and the tests in `tests/test_record.py`; `README.md` now names `record.html` among the pack's files.

**Fix to the v2 table (`src/cma/rules/v2.yaml`).** Lines are now classified in two stages, as the guidance asked:
1. **Line type first:** a returned direct debit, then an explicit move to or from the applicant's own pot, space or savings, then HMRC, then a refund or reversal.
2. **Name lists second:** lenders and MCA providers, director or capital wording, cash places, rent, acquirers, gateways, marketplaces and delivery platforms.

So `TFR FROM SAVINGS CAPITAL RESERVE` is now an own-account transfer, and `CARD REFUND CAPITAL OFFICE SUPPLIES` is a refund. Savings wording with no move (`DIRECTOR SAVINGS`) still counts as director funding. HMRC is checked before the refund wording, so an HMRC refund stays an HMRC repayment.

**Payment rails** (Faster Payment, BACS, CHAPS and so on) don't get a category of their own. A rail anywhere in the line rules out cash; otherwise the name list decides, so `FPS STRIPE` is still a gateway payout. Flagging an acquirer credit that arrives by Faster Payment under a person's name is an indicator's job (R14), not this task's.

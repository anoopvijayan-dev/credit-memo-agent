**How R38 is met**
- **Named profile or custom mapping:** `--format bank-csv:PROFILE` loads one of the four packaged profiles (barclays, lloyds, natwest, starling). `--format bank-csv --mapping FILE` loads a firm's own mapping. Both are checked by `check_mapping`.
- **Paid-in and paid-out columns:** the two unsigned columns are read exactly in pence. A row with an amount in both, or in neither, is refused.
- **Non-ISO dates:** read with the mapping's formats, with English month names whatever the machine's locale.
- **Newest first:** detected from the dates, or from the bank's balances when the first and last rows share a date. A declared order that the dates contradict is refused.
- **Same checks as the five-column loader:** this holds by construction. `contract.write_statement` (`src/cma/importers/contract.py:127`) passes the converted bytes through `ingest.load_csv` before anything is written. `store.import_file` checks them again (`src/cma/store.py:654`).
- **Golden file per profile:** all 54 tests in `tests/test_import_bank_csv.py` show as PASSED in the runner's full-suite and contracts logs. That covers the four profiles, the custom mapping, and the same files through `cma case import`.

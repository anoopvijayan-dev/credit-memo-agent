**Checklist results:**

- **Errors (done):** a request naming no figure or cell gets a 400 that repeats only what was asked. A new test shows an unexpected failure gets a 500 with nothing from the case in it.

- **Observability (not applicable):** the server keeps no request log on purpose, because the request line carries the token. Reads write no case-log event.

That misses the 2-second target at 10,000 lines (R52), and the design says lines are paged in the workspace. Paging needs a design decision, so I left it for the owner.

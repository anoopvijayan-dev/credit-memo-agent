I walked every checklist line for the Open Banking importer and found one real bug, now fixed. The search that orders lines booked at the same moment was recursive and scanned every remaining line at each step.

- **Crash:** a group of about 1,000 or more such lines crashed with `RecursionError` instead of being refused. A date-only export gives exactly that, with a whole day at midnight.
- **Valid files refused:** 300 to 500 same-moment lines written in reverse order hit the 100,000-step cap and the file was refused.

The search is now iterative, looks up which line can come next instead of scanning, and keeps the step cap. Every probe that failed before now orders correctly, and a group whose balances cannot chain is refused, never crashed. I added four contract tests for this, committed as `f1cbec0`. On the final code the full suite passes (2491 tests), and ruff and compileall are clean.

T20 is built and committed as 8188886. Clicking any figure, money-in bucket or monthly cell in the workspace now opens exactly the lines behind it, and the lines' total equals the figure. The full suite passes: 2136 tests, and lint is clean.

- **What each line shows:** date, description, amount, balance, category, and the rule or override that set the category. An overridden line shows its override as `override:N`. Lines come in the same order the figures layer cites them.
- **Figures that add up lines** (M2, M17, M18, M19–M25, and the monthly money-in and trading-receipts cells): the lines' signed total is shown, and it equals the figure to the penny.
- **Money paid out** (M8, M11, M12, M14, and monthly money out): the total is shown and labelled as the figure negated.
- **Averages, shares, ratios and counts:** these show their inputs and their method. Inputs include the figures used, the period, a share's top and bottom amounts, the proposed repayment for M28, and the end-of-day balances read for M6, M27 and the monthly balance cells. M9 also names the lines it counts only in part.
- **Where it lives:** two new endpoints, `/api/lines` for the data and `/lines` for the page, each taking an account plus either a figure or a month and cell. The token is required as for every other request. A request that names nothing valid gets a 400. A case whose money-in buckets don't reconcile shows no lines.
- **Arithmetic:** every total and input is computed in `metrics.py`. The new `src/cma/review/drilldown.py` only assembles and renders the results.

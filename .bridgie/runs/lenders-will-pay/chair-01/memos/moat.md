## What is strong
- **A small market you can name.** The bet starts with UK non-bank SME lenders and NACFB commercial-finance brokers, and banks are listed as a non-goal. The research counts 1,414 NACFB member firms (Jan 2026). That is small enough to cover by hand. Two of the US players are absent: the research found no UK customer for Kaaj and no UK office or expansion news for Ocrolus.
- **A gap in what competitors say.** In the research, the only competitor page that claims anything like "every figure cited and human-approved" is Crediflow's reported source traceability. Ocrolus, Codat, Equifax Share by Bank and Plaid LendScore 2 stop at data or a score, not a memo.
- **A possible secret, untested.** D4 and D11 note that limited-company lending is outside FCA regulation (FCA response to the FSB super-complaint). The pressure for traceability may come from warehouse funders, British Business Bank guarantee-scheme audits or credit committees rather than a regulator. If that is true and few vendors see it, funders could become the sales channel: one funder points several lenders at the same tool. The research did not confirm that BBB audits credit files. Right now this is a hypothesis, not an asset.
- **A channel that could reach many brokers at once.** If brokers turn out to be the buyer, NACFB gathers them in one place (the LendInvest survey was run at the NACFB Commercial Finance Expo). Whether a vendor can reach members through it is unknown.

## What would kill it
1. **Crediflow already holds the UK position with the same claim.** The research says Crediflow released a spreading tool in June 2026 and a credit-memo generator in October 2026. The memo generator fills each lender's own template and has source traceability. It names AddWorking Capital and Iveco Capital as customers and reportedly raised a £1.5m seed round. Against that, "cited and human-approved" is at best slightly better, and slightly better loses to the incumbent. *Test:* in each of the 5 interviews, ask "Have you seen or trialled Crediflow, Kaaj or Arya Cred AI? What was missing?" If 2 or more have evaluated Crediflow and name no gap, the edge isn't there.
2. **The memo step gets absorbed by the data and workflow vendors lenders already pay.** Codat (with its Plaid partnership), Equifax Share by Bank, Validis, Credit Kudos and nCino (Shawbrook) already sit in the lender's stack and control the channel into it (research, Indirect and substitutes; D3). Adding memo drafting is a small step for them, and Plaid shipped LendScore 2 on 6 Oct 2026. Selling memo drafting from outside that stack gets harder as they move up. *Test:* in each interview, ask which data vendor and loan-origination system the lender uses, and whether that vendor has offered memo drafting.
3. **Nothing builds up that stops a copy next year.** The method (deterministic metrics, citations, a sign-off step) can be copied, and Crediflow has already done it. The bet names no data asset; statements belong to the client, and D7 says the data-protection basis isn't drafted. It names no switching cost and no licence. Distribution is the owner sending messages to 20 named contacts, which doesn't get cheaper with scale. The owner's own network may be off-limits: the employment contract has an IP clause and the employer works in merchant-funding risk (D7). *Test:* read the actual contract clauses. Pull the reply rate from the BridgeTrade 2026 outreach log. Name what the product would keep after each memo (lender policy rules, a template library, exception precedents) that makes the 10th customer's memo better than the 1st.
